About

  • Albert Einstein was a theoretical physicist born in March of 1879. Most know him for his work in developing the theory of relativity. He has many important contributions to science and has won multiple prizes including the Nobel Prize in Physics. Einstein graduated from the federal polytechnic school in 1900 and wrote his first scientific paper in 1901 called "Folgerungen aus den Capillartätserscheinungen" where he suggested a model of intermolecular attraction that he later rejected. Einstein has had a multitude of interesting life experiences, including being drafted for military service and renouncing his German citizenship. Throughout his whole life he was a pacifist, with a total aversion to violence.
